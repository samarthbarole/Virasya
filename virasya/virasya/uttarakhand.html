THE ESSENCE OF UTTARAKHAND

A land where nature, spirituality and tradition live together.

⛰

HIMALAYAN LANDSCAPES

Snow-covered peaks of Nanda Devi, peaceful alpine valleys and breathtaking Himalayan natural sanctuary.

🏛

SPIRITUAL HERITAGE

Ancient Char Dham shrines and sacred river confluences connecting millennia of Sanatana Dharma.

♬

RICH CULTURE

Soulful Jhora, Chholiya sword dances, and Pahadi folk music singing of mountain legends and deities.

✥

ARTS & CRAFTS

Sacred Aipan floor art, ringal bamboo craftsmanship, intricate Pahadi wood carvings, and wool weaving.

🌿

SIMPLE LIVING

Warm-hearted communities with pure mountain hospitality and a deep reverence for nature.

✦

CULTURE & TRADITIONS

Stories, traditions and artistic expressions of the Himalayas.

FOLK DANCE

Traditional Chholiya and Jhora dances that celebrate community, valor and devotion.

TRADITIONAL ATTIRE

Handwoven Pichhora, Ghagras, and woollen garments reflecting Himalayan identity.

FOLK MUSIC

Melodies played on Dhol-Damau, Ransingha, and flute carrying the soul of the mountains.

HANDICRAFTS

Handmade Aipan art, Ringal bamboo, and copperware created by skilled local artisans.

❖

FESTIVALS OF UTTARAKHAND

Celebrations that bring communities together in joy and prayer.

NANDA DEVI RAJ JAT

A sacred 3-week royal Himalayan pilgrimage celebrated every 12 years with deep devotion.

HARELA

A festival celebrating nature, green harvest, monsoon arrival, and family prosperity.

KEDARNATH YATRA

A spiritual journey through snow-clad peaks that purifies the mind and spirit.

GANGA DUSSEHRA

Celebration of the descent of Mother Ganga with grand evening Maha Aartis in Haridwar.

✦ ❖ ✦

HERITAGE OF UTTARAKHAND

Sacred places and historic architectural treasures of Devbhoomi.

KEDARNATH TEMPLE

One of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva situated dramatically beneath the Kedarnath peak at 3,583 meters.

BADRINATH

An ancient Mahavishnu temple along the Alaknanda River, surrounded by the Nar and Narayana mountain ranges.

VALLEY OF FLOWERS

A UNESCO World Heritage alpine wonderland carpeted in endemic flora and pristine high-altitude glaciers.
